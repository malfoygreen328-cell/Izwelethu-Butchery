How it would work

From browsing to booked braai

The concept keeps the customer in control: browse, build the order, then send it through WhatsApp where the business confirms stock and the final price.

Discover

Customers find Izwelethu through search, social media or a shared link — and land on products, not a brochure.

Build the order

Cuts, packs and bulk boxes are added to a cart that remembers quantities and survives a page refresh.

Send on WhatsApp

One tap sends a clean, readable order list to the business — no app download, no account, no friction.

Confirm, collect, return

Izwelethu confirms availability and the final price, then the customer collects or receives the order.

Concept only. Live payment, accounts and delivery tracking are not part of this demo. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with a prepared message — the order is only confirmed once Izwelethu replies.
