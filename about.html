What the business does

Retail, wholesale and experience

Public information describes Izwelethu as a butchery, eatery and shisanyama operating in wholesale and retail. The concepts on this site are built around those three parts.

Meat retail

A browsable catalogue of cuts and packs, with a cart that survives a refresh and sends the order on WhatsApp.

Wholesale

A business enquiry flow that captures volumes and produces quote requests for repeat supply.

Shisanyama experience

A page for the fire, the food and the gathering — menu and events supplied by the business.
